Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to StudentHub?

Use your enrollment number and password provided by the college.

How can I check my attendance?

After logging in, open the Attendance page from the Dashboard.

Where can I view my timetable?

The weekly timetable is available on the Timetable page.

How do I check my exam results?

Visit the Performance page to view your marks, grades, SGPA and CGPA.

How can I know about upcoming college events?

All workshops, seminars, sports activities and cultural programs are listed on the Events page.

Who should I contact for technical support?

Use the Contact page to reach the Student Support Team.

Can I access StudentHub from my mobile phone?

Yes. StudentHub can be accessed using a mobile phone, tablet or computer.

Need More Help?

If your question is not listed above, please visit the Contact page or email us at support@charusat.edu.
